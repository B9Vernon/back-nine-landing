# GLOBAL OUTLOOK — BASELINE v1 (2026-07-10)
Confidence: MODERATE (panel seeded, not yet live-verified).

Strongest conclusion: Electricity demand growth has structurally resumed in North America
after two flat decades (AI/data centres, electrification, reshoring). Regulated wires/
generation asset bases grow regardless of which AI company wins.
Most important disagreement: Whether AI capex is durable or an overbuild cycle; a capex
bust would hit merchant power and data-centre suppliers far harder than regulated utilities.
Most investable structural implication: Own the bottlenecks — regulated grid assets, gas
transmission, generation capacity, transformer/electrical equipment — not the headline AI names.
Most important uncertainty: Interest-rate path; every income asset in the mandate is
rate-sensitive, and sustained high rates compress both valuations and split-share/leveraged-fund economics.
