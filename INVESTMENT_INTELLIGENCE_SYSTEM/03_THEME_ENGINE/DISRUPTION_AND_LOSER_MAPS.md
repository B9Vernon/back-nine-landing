# DISRUPTION AND LOSER MAPS (v1)
- Single-stock option-income ETFs (MSTY/MSTE/NVHE/LLHE class): structurally lose if underlying
  volatility falls OR underlying crashes. Product class, not business, bears the risk.
- Long-duration bond CC funds (HPYT): capped recovery upside; lose in both rate directions asymmetrically.
- Cannabis (ACB): structural oversupply, excise burden, chronic dilution — no 10-year income case.
- Legacy telcos/media: rate-sensitive dividends w/o growth (BCE-type) — treat high yields skeptically.
- Office real estate: structural demand loss.
