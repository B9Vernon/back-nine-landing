# INVESTMENT MANDATE
Style: long-term, income-oriented, concentrated, high-conviction, low-turnover, reinvest & compound.
Universe: securities listed on recognized Canadian or U.S. exchanges (research is global).
Objective: strongest sustainable combination of income, dividend growth, capital preservation,
total return, compounding, valuation, resilience, understandability, portfolio fit.
NOT the objective: maximum headline yield.
Non-negotiables: no automatic trading; all decisions remain the investor's; never confuse
advertised yield with quality; "do nothing" is a valid, successful decision; recommend rarely.

## Three timeless filters (all must pass for high conviction)
1. DO I KNOW ABOUT IT? — understandable business, within natural information flow
2. WILL I PAY ATTENTION TO IT? — meaningful position, worth monitoring
3. WILL IT STILL BE TRUE IN ~10 YEARS? — structural demand, durable asset/bottleneck, sustainable FCF
Grades per filter: Pass / Conditional pass / Fail / Unknown.
