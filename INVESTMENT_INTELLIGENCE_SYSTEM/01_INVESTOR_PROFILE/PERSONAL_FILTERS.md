# PERSONAL FILTERS — APPLICATION NOTES
Filter 1 (Know it): Investor demonstrably follows: income ETFs, covered-call mechanics,
Canadian energy, utilities/electricity, US mega-cap tech, crypto income products, real
estate, banks, infrastructure. Businesses far outside these (e.g., specialty pharma,
biotech) start at Research Only regardless of quality.
Filter 2 (Attention): Prefers concentrated meaningful positions. Reject tiny starter
sprinkles. Monitoring burden counts against candidates in fast-moving niches.
Filter 3 (10-year truth): Structures depending on sustained high volatility (single-stock
option-income funds), temporary scarcity pricing, or refinancing luck normally FAIL.
Regulated assets, scarce infrastructure, royalty/fee models normally PASS.
