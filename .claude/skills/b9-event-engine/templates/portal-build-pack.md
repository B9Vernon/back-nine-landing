5. **Competitive hook**

   `poster-brief.md`)
7. **In-depth sales-focused description** (11 sections per `../references/sales-copy.md`)
8. **Prize and reward section**
9. **Three-step registration section**
10. **Golf Canada app Record & Reward section**
11. **Secondary membership section**
12. **Complete portal-ready HTML** (full fragment, `../references/html-builder.md`)
13. **Image replacement list** (every image placeholder → what Vernon must supply)
14. **Link replacement list** (every link placeholder → the exact URL needed)

16. **Short consistency check** (one-paragraph result of the QC pass)
17. **Recommended connections** (specific asks that would improve accuracy or future
    builds, per `../references/connections.md` — or "none needed")
