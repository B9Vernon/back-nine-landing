# Poster & Hero Image Creation

Every build includes a poster or hero-image deliverable.

## Approved source material

Back Nine logos · facility photos · simulator photos · course images · seasonal
images · sponsor logos · previous poster references. In-repo assets available now:
`landing-page-files/assets/` (logo.png, swing-hero.jpg, swing-b9wall.jpg,
swing-data.jpg, tournaments-widget-corp.jpg, and more) and
`NIB2/public/assets/b9-emblem.png`. These are local files — for portal HTML they must
be hosted at public HTTPS URLs (ask Vernon or use placeholders).

## When image-generation tools are available

Create the finished asset directly, following the Back Nine visual standard (see
`html-builder.md` palette and the brand rules in
`.claude/skills/cinematic-prompt-architect/references/back-nine-brand.md` — premium,
dark, modern; never cheap, cluttered, arcade-like, or neon-flooded; exact logo
proportions; one golf ball maximum in any golf imagery).

## When direct generation is unavailable — deliver a complete brief

Use `../templates/poster-brief.md`. It must include: exact dimensions · complete design
brief · visual hierarchy · text placement · colour instructions · asset list · crop
instructions · a ready-to-run image-generation prompt · export specifications.

## Poster content (normally)

event name · event theme · dates · player format (when supplied) · Back Nine Vernon
branding · strong competitive imagery · prize message when appropriate.

## Rules

- Must remain readable displayed small (portal thumbnail test: legible at ~300px wide).
- Standard sizes: portal poster 1080×1350 (4:5) unless the portal dictates otherwise;
  optional social square 1080×1080 and story 1080×1920 variants.
- Never use protected professional golf photography, tournament logos, tournament
  names, or player likenesses without permission.
- Record approved/rejected visual styles in `../memory/preferences.md`.
