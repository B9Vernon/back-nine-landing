# Golf Canada App — Record and Reward Section

Every full build includes an app-first Golf Canada section. **Back Nine Indoor Golf —
Vernon** is listed as an indoor facility through Golf Canada — that exact facility name
must appear in the section.

## The behaviour to drive

1. Finish the league or tournament round.
2. Open the Golf Canada app on your phone.
3. Select **Back Nine Indoor Golf — Vernon**.
4. Record the indoor round or practice session.
5. Do it before leaving the facility.
6. Get friends, family, partners, and teammates to record theirs too.

## Urgency built on ease, not pressure

- "Record it before you leave the bay."
- "It takes about 10 seconds."
- "Finish the round, open the app, and lock it into your golf history."
- "Get your whole team to record today's session."

## CTAs the section supports

- **Clickable Golf Canada logo** (Vernon-mandated) at the top of the section, linking to
  the app. The official logo image isn't in Vernon's branding files and can't be fetched
  from this environment — until Vernon supplies it, use the clickable
  "⛳ Golf Canada — Open the App" badge; when he provides the logo file/URL, embed it as
  base64 and wrap the `<img>` in the same link.
- `DOWNLOAD FOR IPHONE` → `https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/golf-canada-mobile/id635386429` (verified)
- `DOWNLOAD FOR ANDROID` → `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=air.com.MobileApp.GolfNet.CanadaPro` (verified)
- QR code for desktop viewers (`{{GOLF_CANADA_QR_IMAGE_URL}}` or ask Vernon) · exact
  facility name · reward info · group-challenge wording when relevant.

## Rewards

When Vernon confirms a participation reward, promote it (`{{GOLF_CANADA_REWARD}}`).
Possible forms: merchandise · practice credit · event prize eligibility · priority
registration · participation bonus · team challenge reward. NEVER invent the reward.

## Compliance — never claim

- that simulator activity affects the official Handicap Index
- that recording guarantees algorithmic placement
- that Golf Canada guarantees exposure
- endorsement beyond the confirmed facility listing

## Approved framing

> Recording your session helps keep your indoor and outdoor golf activity connected and
> supports Back Nine Vernon's presence within Canada's growing off-course golf community.

## Hierarchy

Secondary to event registration, but visually prominent — its own coloured block, never
a footnote.
