# Prize and Competitive Reward Copy

Every event communicates that players may compete for: cash · a share of a purse ·
merchandise · gift cards · sponsor prizes · golf time · trophies · recognition ·
additional rewards · prizes to be announced.

## Placement — the prize message appears three times

1. Near the opening of the description.
2. In a dedicated visual section (its own coloured block in the HTML).
3. Near the final registration CTA.

## When prize details are confirmed by Vernon

Promote them clearly and specifically. Use exactly the values supplied — never rounded,
inflated, or embellished.

## When details are NOT confirmed — approved fallback copy

> **PLAY FOR MORE THAN BRAGGING RIGHTS**
>
> Prizes and competitive rewards will be on the line. Enter the field, compete through
> every round, and give yourself the chance to finish on top. Full prize details will be
> announced before the competition begins.

(May be lightly adapted in tone to fit the event theme; the meaning must not change.)

## Never

- invent prize values, purse percentages, merchandise values, sponsor contributions,
  or guaranteed winnings
- imply that every participant wins
- fabricate sponsor commitments

## Emotional principle

**You cannot win if you are not in the field.** This is the engine of the prize
message — entry buys the chance, competition earns the reward.

## Optional sponsor ideas (only when suitable — keep brief)

Suggest at most a few categories: title sponsor · prize sponsor · finals sponsor ·
closest-to-the-pin sponsor · merchandise sponsor · hospitality sponsor. Do not build a
full sponsorship system unless requested.
