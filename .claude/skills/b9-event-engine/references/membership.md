# Membership Pathway (Secondary CTA)

Where appropriate, include a short secondary membership section. The event always
remains the main focus — the membership CTA must never overpower event registration.

**Verified URL:** https://backninegolf.ca/local/vernonbc/memberships/

## CTA wording options

- Explore Memberships
- Make Back Nine Your Golf Home
- Play and Practise Year-Round
- Find Your Membership

## Angle

Show naturally that regular competitors benefit from year-round access and ongoing
practice: the golfer who loved competing this season is the golfer who practises
between events — and members practise on their schedule, 24/7. One short block, one
button, quieter styling than the registration CTAs.
