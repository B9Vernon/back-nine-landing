# Concept Engine — Original Competition Design

Create event concepts that give golfers a powerful reason to register and compete.
These directions are creative starting points, not fixed templates.

## League directions

individual season league · two-person team league · scramble league · match-play league ·
skins league · elimination league · points-race league · major-championship series ·
Ryder-style team competition · business-versus-business league · couples league ·
women's league · beginner-friendly league · senior league · member-guest league ·
city-versus-city competition · nine-hole league · limited-club challenge ·
mystery-course league · qualifier-and-playoff league · seasonal championship.

## Tournament directions

one-day major · multi-day major · weekend scramble · knockout championship · team cup ·
member-guest tournament · corporate challenge · seasonal championship · qualifier and
live final · holiday tournament · charity competition · professional-golf-inspired
event · closest-to-the-pin championship · long-drive competition · short-format
shootout · survival-style tournament · championship series.

## Every event must feel different through

competitive structure · theme · emotional hook · visual identity · seasonal relevance ·
professional-golf connection · prize positioning · reason to return · reason to win.

## Originality rules

- Do not repeatedly rename the same event. A new coat of paint on last season's format
  is not a new concept — change the structure or the hook, not just the name.
- Before proposing names or themes, check `../memory/event-log.md` for names, visual
  themes, seasonal themes, and pro-golf themes already used.
- Weak concepts flagged in memory are off the table unless Vernon revives them.
- Every IDEAS batch should span genuinely different competitive structures (e.g. one
  points race, one match-play bracket, one team format, one skills-based event — not
  four stroke-play variants).

## Emotional targets

Sell competition, rivalry, achievement, community, social connection, year-round golf,
prizes, bragging rights, repeat participation, and the feeling of being part of a real
event. The registration decision is emotional first, logistical second.
