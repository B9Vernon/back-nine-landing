# In-Depth Sales Description

Every full build includes a detailed, persuasive event description. It must never read
like an ordinary event notice.

## The reader must feel

they want to be part of the competition · the event is worth paying for · they could
miss something memorable · they have a real chance to compete and win · their friends,
family, coworkers, or partner should join them · Back Nine Vernon is THE place for
local indoor competition · they will want to return for the next event · regular
participation leads naturally toward membership.

## Voice

Energetic · competitive · premium · welcoming · confident · emotional · clear ·
sales-focused · urgent without fake pressure. Short, visual sections — no walls of
text. Premium, never cheap or gimmicky; matches the Back Nine brand (see
`poster-visuals.md` for visual identity notes).

## Required structure — every full description, in order

1. **Strong opening hook** — competitive, emotional, specific to this event.
2. **Event overview** — what it is, when, how it works (only Vernon-supplied facts or
   placeholders).
3. **Why players will want in** — the reasons this one is unmissable.
4. **Competitive experience** — what competing actually feels like week to week or
   round to round.
5. **Social and community value** — who they'll play with, rivalries, atmosphere.
6. **Prize and reward section** — per `prize-copy.md`.
7. **Important event information** — dates, deadline, fees, format, limits
   (supplied values or placeholders only).
8. **Three-step registration section** — per `three-step-registration.md`.
9. **Golf Canada app section** — per `golf-canada.md`.
10. **Final urgency and registration CTA** — real scarcity only (limited field, real
    deadline), never fabricated pressure.
11. **Secondary membership pathway** — per `membership.md`.

## Final gate

Silently ask: *Would this make a golfer stop, read, click, pay, and want to compete?*
If not, rewrite before delivering. Watch memory notes for "too long" / "too short"
feedback and calibrate length accordingly.
