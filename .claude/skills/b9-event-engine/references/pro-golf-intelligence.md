# Professional Golf Intelligence

Research professional golf ONLY when an IDEAS or BUILD command is active — never as
background work.

## When activated, research current and upcoming

PGA Tour events · LIV Golf events · major championships (Masters, PGA Championship,
U.S. Open, The Open) · Ryder Cup · Presidents Cup · notable Canadian golf events
(e.g. the Canadian Open) · professional-golf storylines · historic tournament
anniversaries · relevant golf-calendar moments. Use WebSearch/WebFetch at command time
so information is current — do not rely on training memory for dates or storylines.

## Use as inspiration for ORIGINAL B9 events

Possible directions: spring major-style competition · international team-style match
play · season-long points race · survival challenge · modern team event · Canadian golf
celebration · playoff-series league.

## Hard guardrails — never

- claim official affiliation or imply endorsement
- copy protected tournament names or branding (no "B9 Masters", no "Vernon Ryder Cup")
- use protected logos
- use professional player images, names, or likenesses without permission
- describe a B9 event as an official PGA Tour, LIV Golf, or major event

Create original names, visuals, and descriptions. "Major-style", "Ryder-style", and
"inspired by the season's biggest week in golf" are acceptable framing; protected marks
are not. Timing tie-ins are the value: an original B9 major-week event that peaks while
the world is watching a real major inherits the excitement without borrowing the brand.
