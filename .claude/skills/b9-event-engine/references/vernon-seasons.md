# Vernon Seasonal Intelligence

Every idea and build must account for Vernon, BC's four distinct seasons. When
activated, determine: current season · approaching season · local weather patterns ·
likely outdoor-course availability · daylight length · holiday periods · wildfire-smoke
risk (typically mid-late summer) · indoor-golf demand · whether the event should be
short, social, flexible, or commitment-heavy. Think one season ahead: a build delivered
late in a season should usually target the next one.

Use real seasonal context. Never invent weather urgency.

## WINTER (peak indoor season — outdoor courses closed, long nights, holidays)

Prioritize: longer leagues · points races · match-play seasons · team championships ·
winter cups · simulator majors · corporate leagues · recurring weekly competition.
Golfers commit deeply in winter — commitment-heavy formats work.

## SPRING (outdoor season approaching — anticipation is the hook)

Prioritize: outdoor-season preparation · season-opening tournaments · tune-up leagues ·
major-inspired events · early-season challenges · competitive momentum. Message: arrive
at the first outdoor tee sharp, not rusty.

## SUMMER (outdoor competition, long days — indoor is the escape hatch)

Prioritize: shorter tournaments · evening competitions · flexible completion windows ·
smoke-day and weather-proof events · social formats · major-championship tie-ins ·
lower-commitment events. Air-conditioned, smoke-free, dark-out-never matters. Do not
ask summer golfers for a 12-week commitment.

## FALL (transition back indoors — the indoor season launch)

Prioritize: transition-back-indoors leagues · team cups · fall championships ·
playoff-style events · indoor-season launch tournaments · winter-league previews.
Capture outdoor rivalries as they move inside.

## Vernon calendar anchors (verify dates when activated)

BC Family Day (Feb) · Spring Break (Mar) · Easter · Victoria Day (May) · Canada Day
(Jul 1) · BC Day (Aug) · Labour Day (Sep) · Thanksgiving (Oct, Canadian) · Halloween ·
Remembrance Day (Nov 11) · Christmas/New Year. Okanagan wildfire-smoke season is
typically Jul-Sep — a legitimate, factual reason indoor golf wins on smoke days.
