Outside Is Closing. The Bracket Is Opening.

The mornings are getting colder, the tee sheets are getting shorter, and every outdoor round starts to feel like the last one. Good — because when the frost shows up, the real season starts: indoors, head-to-head, win or go home.

FIRST FROST MATCH PLAY is a knockout bracket played entirely at Back Nine Vernon. Beat the player in front of you and advance. Lose, and you're a spectator. Book each match on your own schedule inside the round window — no excuses, no second chances. Rewards are on the line.

What You're Signing Up For

Win or Go Home
Every match is elimination. No grinding for points, no safe middle of the pack — you take your opponent out or you're done.
Play Your Match on Your Time
Each bracket round has a booking window. You and your opponent pick the bay time that works — the pressure is scheduled, the excuses aren't.
The Bracket Tells the Story
Watch every upset as the field shrinks round by round. Everyone in the building knows exactly who's still alive.

Say Goodbye to Daylight Golf

Take one last look at the sunset rounds — then bring your game inside, where the season never ends. The bracket doesn't care what the weather is doing.
