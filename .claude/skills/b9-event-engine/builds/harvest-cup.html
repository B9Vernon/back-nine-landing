HARVEST CUP

Vernon's Fall Team Match Play Cup

Two-Player Teams · Match Play Cup

The Outdoor Season Ends. The Rivalries Don't.

All summer you've been keeping score against the same people — your league mates, your weekend group, the one guy who never stops talking. The courses are about to close on all of it. The Harvest Cup keeps it going.

HARVEST CUP is a two-player team match play cup played as the valley turns gold — the same stretch of fall when golf's biggest team showdown has the whole sport picking sides. Pick your partner, defend your side, and settle the summer's arguments indoors, match by match. Rewards are on the line.

What You're Signing Up For

Pick Your Partner Wisely
Two-player sides, team match play. Your partner's par saves your bogey — and you'll both remember who carried who.
Every Match Means Something
Cup points ride on every session. No throwaway rounds — each match won moves your side up the board.
Fall's Best Reason to Keep Competing
As outdoor golf winds down, the Cup winds up. This is how Vernon's rivalries survive the winter.

Team Golf, the Way It Should Feel

Standing over a putt while your partner and your opponents watch — that's the Cup. Every bay becomes a stage, and every match becomes a story you'll retell all winter.
