CLEAR AIR CLASSIC

The Smoke-Season Scramble

Two-Player Scramble

When the Valley Turns Grey, the Golf Comes Inside.

Every Okanagan golfer knows the drill: mid-summer rolls in, the smoke settles over the valley, and the outdoor round you planned quietly disappears. Your golf doesn't have to go with it.

CLEAR AIR CLASSIC is a two-player scramble played in perfect conditions — air-conditioned, smoke-free, and always green. Grab a partner, book your window, and post a best-ball number while everyone else waits for the sky to clear. Rewards are on the line.

What You're Signing Up For

Grab a Partner, Split the Pressure
Scramble format: both players hit, you play the better ball. Big drives get used, bad swings get forgotten — golf at its most fun.
Perfect Conditions, Guaranteed
No smoke advisories, no 35-degree afternoons, no wind. The Classic plays in the exact same clear air for every team in the field.
One Round, Zero Commitment
A single scramble round inside a flexible window. Fit it around work, the lake, and everything else summer throws at you.

Outside Golf, Without the Outside

Real courses on the screen, real data on every shot, and a fairway that's always in season. When the air outside gets thick, this is where the valley keeps swinging.
