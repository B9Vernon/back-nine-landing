The Mountain Moves Because You Do

Now Give the Entire Mountain Something to Talk About.

The Mountain Crew Cup is a premium indoor golf league created exclusively for SilverStar staff. Every round carries the name and personality of a familiar SilverStar ski run. Every score pushes your crew higher or sends a rival past you.

Flexible playing windows make it work around early starts, closing shifts, weekends, weather and the glorious chaos of resort life. New golfers are welcome. Experienced golfers should expect to be hunted.
