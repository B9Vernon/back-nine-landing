The Mountain Moves Because You Do

Now Give the Entire Mountain Something to Talk About.

The Mountain Crew Cup is a premium indoor golf league created exclusively for SilverStar staff. Every round carries the name and personality of a familiar SilverStar ski run. Every score pushes your crew higher or sends a rival past you.

Flexible playing windows make it work around early starts, closing shifts, weekends, weather and the glorious chaos of resort life. New golfers are welcome. Experienced golfers should expect to be hunted.

THE SIX ROUNDS

OPENING RUN · BLUE

The season opener rewards a clean start and a steady card. No league is won in Round One, but plenty of people begin digging a hole here. Tap again to close.

MOMENTUM RUN · BLUE

+
Round Two is where a good start becomes a run—or a rough opener becomes a comeback. Every shot begins carrying a little more weight.
03
MOVING DAY · BLUE
ROLLER COASTER
Expect climbs, drops and a standings shake-up.
+
The middle of the schedule is built for movement. One great round can launch a crew; one ugly hole can make the rest of the season interesting.
04
PRESSURE RUN · BLUE
CHRISTMAS BOWL
The gift is opportunity. The pressure is using it.
+
The field is taking shape. This is the round where contenders stop looking accidental and every missed chance feels bigger.
05
CUTLINE RUN · BLACK
HEADWALL
No cruising. No hiding. Earn the final.
+
The penultimate round turns up the pressure. Protecting a position is dangerous; attacking one is worse. Exactly as it should be.
06
CHAMPIONSHIP RUN · GOLD
ELDORADO
The longest road. The final card. The Cup.
+
Everything built across five rounds lands here. The season closes, the standings lock, and one SilverStar crew reaches the summit.
