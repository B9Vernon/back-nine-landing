TWO BALL SOCIAL

The Couples & Partners League

Two-Player Teams · Social 9-Hole League

Golf Night, Date Night, League Night — One Booking.

Finding one night a week that works for both of you is hard enough. Two Ball Social makes it easy: nine holes, one shared ball, and a room full of other teams doing exactly the same thing.

TWO BALL SOCIAL is a relaxed two-player league where partners alternate shots — couples, friends, siblings, whoever you trust with your drive. Nine holes a night, standings all season, zero pressure to be perfect. Rewards are on the line.

What You're Signing Up For

One Ball, Two Players
Alternate-shot means every hole is a conversation. You'll celebrate their putts, forgive their chunks, and argue strategy all the way home.
Nine Holes, No Hurry
A single nine each week keeps the night short and social — play, laugh, grab a drink in the lounge, and let the standings sort themselves out.
A League for Every Skill Level
Alternate-shot is the great equalizer. New golfers get carried, good golfers get humbled, and every team has a real shot.

The League Night That Feels Like a Night Out

Premium bays, a comfortable lounge, and music on — Two Ball Social is as much about the hang as the golf. Come for the league, stay for the atmosphere.
