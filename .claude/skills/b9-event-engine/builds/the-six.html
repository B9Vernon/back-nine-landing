THE SIX

Indoor Season League

Six rounds. One season table. No hiding.

1ST, 2ND & 3RD PLACE ALL WIN CASH PRIZES

Six rounds to prove it.

THE SIX is a six-round indoor golf league built for players who want more than a casual simulator round. Every stop puts a new course in front of you, every score adds pressure to the table, and every round gives you another chance to move.

The format is simple to follow and hard to fake: show up, post your round, track the standings, and keep chasing the top. First, second, and third place will each receive a cash prize.
