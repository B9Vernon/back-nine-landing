This Is Your League. Your Leaderboard. Your Season.

No waiting on a tee sheet that never opens up. No squeezing onto a course that wasn't built with you in mind. The Birdie League is six rounds of real competition — built for women who want to play, improve, and win, on their own schedule, in their own space.

THE BIRDIE LEAGUE brings Vernon's women golfers together for a full six-round season on the simulator. Every round posts to a live leaderboard, every week you climb, and one player finishes on top. Brand new to the game or already breaking 90 — you belong in this field, and rewards are on the line.
