When The Season Ends Out There

The Courses Close.
Your Season Doesn't.

Frost on the fairways. Carts parked. Everyone else puts the clubs in the garage until spring — and that is exactly when THE LONG GAME tees off. Ten rounds with your partner, played in a warm bay while it snows outside.

Two Players. One Ball. Ten Rounds to Get It Right.

A scramble is the best format in golf for a reason: nobody plays alone. Your partner bails you out of the trees, you bail them out of the bunker, and every good shot either of you hits is a shot you both get to use.

Stretch that over ten rounds and a whole winter and it stops being a one-off. It becomes a season — a partnership with a record, a rhythm, and something real to chase all the way to the last round. Find your partner. Play the long game.

How A Scramble Works

Never played one? It takes about ten seconds to learn.

1Both of You Tee Off
Every hole starts with two drives — two chances at the fairway.
2Take the Better Ball
Pick whichever shot you like best. Bad swings simply disappear.
3Both Play From There
You both hit the next one from that spot. Repeat until it drops.

What You're Signing Up For

A Partner, Not A Scorecard
Golf gets lonely when it's just you and a bad round. Here, every hole is shared — two drives, two chances, and someone in the bay who actually wants you to make the putt.
Winter Is The Whole Point
While everyone else waits out the snow, you're playing ten rounds in a warm bay with perfect lies. You'll come out of winter sharper than the people who took it off.
Beginners Welcome, Genuinely
The scramble format hides a rough swing better than any other game in golf. Bring a partner who's never played — they'll contribute more than they expect.

Perfect Lies. Perfect Weather. Every Round.

Pro-level ball tracking, a warm bay, and a course that's always in mid-summer condition — at 7 in the morning or 11 at night. Book your round when it suits the two of you, not when the weather allows it.

Who Are You Bringing?

Your spouse. Your best friend. The buddy who keeps saying he'll come out sometime. Your dad. Your kid. Ten rounds together over one winter is the easiest way to turn we should golf more into a standing date the two of you actually keep.
