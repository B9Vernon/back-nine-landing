The Best Golf in Vernon Happens After Dinner.

Summer days are packed — work, the lake, the yard, the kids. But the evenings? The evenings are yours. And Back Nine Vernon doesn't close.

AFTER DARK SKINS is a weekly evening skins league built for golfers whose free time starts when the sun gets low. Every hole is worth a skin, ties carry the pot forward, and one great swing can flip the whole night. Show up, cash your moment, come back next week. Rewards are on the line.

What You're Signing Up For

Every Hole Is Its Own Battle
Win the hole outright, win the skin. Tie it, and the skin carries until somebody claims the pile. Nine chances a night to be the hero.
Anyone Can Win Any Week
Skins doesn't care about your scorecard total. One perfect wedge on the right hole beats an evening of steady pars.
Your Week Gets a Standing Game
Same night, every week, all season. It's the regular game your group keeps talking about — actually happening.

Where Golf Never Sleeps

24/7 access means the league fits your life, not the other way around. Cool bays, a premium lounge, and skins on the line while the rest of the valley winds down.
