The Setup

The Pros Are Chasing Points. So Are You.

Every August, professional golf turns into a math problem — points, projections, and one number that decides who gets to play for everything. This year, Vernon gets its own version.

PLAYOFF PUSH is a three-round points race at Back Nine Vernon. Every qualifying round you post earns points. Climb the standings, survive the cut, and earn your bay at Finals Night — one live session where the leaders settle it head-to-head. Rewards are on the line.

What You're Signing Up For
01Three Rounds. Every Point Counts.
Post three qualifying rounds inside the race window. Great rounds bank big points — and grinders can climb on consistency alone.
02Watch the Standings Move
A live points board tracks the whole field. One hot round can launch you up the list. Someone else's can knock you right back down.
03Finals Night Is the Reward
The top of the standings earns a spot at a live, in-person Finals Night — lights on, field watching, everything decided in one session.
Your Numbers Do the Talking

Every qualifying round is tracked shot by shot — ball speed, carry, proximity — plus the only stat that matters in August: where you sit on the points board.
