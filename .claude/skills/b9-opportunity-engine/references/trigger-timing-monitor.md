# D. Trigger & Timing Monitor

Separates evergreen prospects from ones with a reason to write **now**.
Module 3 (community calendar) collects events; this decides which of them
is an outreach trigger and when the window closes.

## Trigger classes

| Trigger | Why it matters | Typical lead time |
|---|---|---|
| Upcoming tournament, league, fundraiser | needs a venue, prizes, or a sponsor | 6–12 weeks |
| Staff appreciation / year-end / holiday party season | books Oct–Dec | 8–14 weeks |
| Conference, AGM, trade show in Vernon | delegates need an evening | 4–10 weeks |
| Grand opening, expansion, relocation, anniversary | wants local goodwill and exposure | 2–6 weeks |
| Hiring push or new-branch announcement | growing headcount, new team to entertain | 2–8 weeks |
| Seasonal slowdown for an outdoor trade | crews free, evenings open | at the turn |
| Tourism shoulder season / weather-contingency | guests need an indoor option | before the season |
| Sponsorship page or vendor call published | an open, dated ask | as posted |

## Rules

- A trigger must be **verified from a dated public source**, with the URL
  retained. Never infer an event, a date, an anniversary or a milestone.
- Record the event date AND the outreach deadline (when the decision is
  actually made — usually well before the event).
- A prospect with no verified trigger is not disqualified; it is evergreen
  and scores 0 on the timing criterion in `partnership-fit-scorer.md`.
- Do not manufacture urgency in the email. Reference the real, verified
  thing or say nothing about timing at all.

## Seasonal shape for Vernon

- **Sep–Nov** — corporate and staff-party booking season; outdoor trades
  winding down; hockey/skating associations mid-season.
- **Dec–Mar** — the facility's strongest stretch; outdoor businesses at
  their quietest; ski traffic through Silver Star.
- **Apr–Jun** — golf season opens, indoor demand dips, leagues wind up,
  sports associations run end-of-season events.
- **Jul–Aug** — tourism peak; accommodation, weather-contingency and
  visitor-activity partnerships are at their most relevant.
