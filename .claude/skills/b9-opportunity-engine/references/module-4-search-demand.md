# Module 4 — Google Local Search Demand Intelligence

Analyze current local search-demand themes for Vernon and the surrounding
area, then convert them into partnership/event targets.

## Sources, strongest-first

Use whatever is actually accessible in the session:

1. Google Keyword Planner / Search Console — only if connected
2. Google Trends (BC / Kelowna-region granularity is usually the floor)
3. Google autocomplete and "related searches" patterns
4. Public Google search results and local-pack patterns
5. Known seasonal behaviour (Okanagan winter/shoulder season, tourism cycles)

## Honesty rule — non-negotiable

Do **not** claim exact Vernon search volumes unless reliable location-specific
data is available. When exact data is unavailable, label the findings:
**"Top verified or high-confidence local search-demand themes."** Mark each
claim's basis (Trends data vs. autocomplete pattern vs. seasonal inference).

## Theme space to explore (plus discover beyond it)

things to do in Vernon · indoor activities · rainy-day activities · winter
activities · evening activities · date-night ideas · group activities ·
corporate events · team-building activities · staff party venues · client
entertainment · birthday venues · family activities · private event venues ·
conference activities · tourism experiences · golf lessons · indoor golf ·
golf memberships · golf leagues · social golf · bachelor/group activities

Also actively look for **unexpected rising or seasonal themes** — new events,
new venues, weather-driven spikes, travel patterns.

## Per-run output

**A. Five strongest demand themes**, each with:
- rising / stable / declining / seasonal
- the likely audience or need behind the search
- how the demand connects to Back Nine Vernon

**B. 10 business suggestions** derived from the demand findings: specific
local businesses or organizations offering a corporate, event, referral,
tourism, or partnership opportunity. For each: the demand theme it rides on,
the suggested opportunity, why the timing makes sense, and the official
website. Verify each business is active before suggesting it.
