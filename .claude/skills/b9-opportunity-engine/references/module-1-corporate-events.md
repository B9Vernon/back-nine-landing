# Module 1 — Corporate Event Finder

Find companies and organizations in the target geography that have a **timely,
publicly visible reason** to organize:

staff appreciation · team building · client entertainment · holiday parties ·
private events · networking events · company celebrations · recurring staff
nights · corporate memberships · employee experiences

## Qualifying public signals

Surface a company only when at least one signal like these is publicly
documented and reasonably current:

- expansion, new location, or major new project
- team growth or visible hiring activity
- anniversary, award, or company milestone
- new ownership, merger/acquisition, or leadership change
- conference participation or event sponsorship
- community involvement or staff-recognition activity
- seasonal staff-event patterns (e.g. holiday party season, summer socials)

**Do not assume every company needs an event.** No signal, no listing.

## How to research

- Use news sources (Castanet Vernon, Vernon Matters, Vernon Morning Star,
  City of Vernon news, press releases) to discover *changes*.
- Use Chamber/DVA directories and business-award pages to confirm the
  organization is active and local.
- Confirm each opportunity against the organization's official website before
  reporting; include that URL.
- Cross-feed: sponsors and organizers found by Module 3 are strong Module 1
  candidates (they demonstrably spend on events).

## For each qualified opportunity, determine

- the signal and why it creates event potential *now*
- the most natural Back Nine offer (team-building night, staff appreciation,
  client entertainment, holiday party, corporate membership, league, etc.)
- the key date (event date, milestone date, seasonal window)
- when Back Nine should act (lead time for corporate bookings is typically
  3–8 weeks; holiday parties book September–October)

Then classify into ACT NOW / COMING SOON / MONITOR per
`timing-and-output.md`.
