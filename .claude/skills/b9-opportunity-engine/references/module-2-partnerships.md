# Module 2 — Partnership Matchmaker

Identify businesses and organizations suited to an **ongoing relationship**
with Back Nine Vernon (not one-off bookings — those belong to Module 1).

## Partnership types to match

- hotel guest referrals; tourism referrals and packages
- restaurant-and-golf or winery-and-golf paired experiences
- employee perks; corporate memberships
- client-entertainment packages (realtors, mortgage/finance, insurance,
  professional services)
- charity events, sponsorships, community collaborations
- reciprocal promotions and cross-promotions
- recurring private bookings and leagues
- association-member experiences; conference add-on activities

## Natural-fit heuristics for Vernon

- **Hotels/resorts** (esp. those hosting winter or shoulder-season guests):
  indoor golf is a rainy-day/winter activity their front desk can recommend.
- **Restaurants, breweries, wineries**: pre/post-golf pairings; each sends
  customers to the other on the same evening.
- **Realtors, mortgage brokers, financial advisors**: client-appreciation
  events are a standard, recurring spend.
- **Ski hill / tourism operators**: complementary weather (bad ski day →
  indoor activity), package potential.
- **Sports associations, minor hockey, curling, golf clubs**: off-season
  training, fundraisers, wind-ups.
- **Chambers, BNI-style groups, associations**: recurring networking venues.

## For each partnership opportunity, determine

- the most natural partnership concept
- the value to **both** parties (be specific about what the partner gains)
- why the timing may be appropriate now
- one-time vs. recurring
- the best time to approach (seasonal windows, before their peak planning
  period, tied to a triggering event)

Confirm the organization is active via its official website; include the URL.
Do not generate outreach emails or marketing copy. Classify results under
PARTNERSHIP POTENTIAL (or MONITOR if not yet ripe).
