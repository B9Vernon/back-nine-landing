# Map Grid Discovery

Divide the area around Back Nine Vernon into small search zones and search
each zone separately, so discovery isn't dominated by the same few obvious
top results.

## Vernon-area grid (starter zones — extend as needed)

- Downtown core: 30th Ave (Main St), 30th St, 31st–33rd Ave cross-blocks
- 32nd Street corridor (Hwy 97 through town)
- 27th Street corridor
- 25th Ave / Polson Park / Polson Place Mall / Hwy 6 East
- Anderson Way / 48th Ave big-box + services zone
- Village Green Centre + 27th St north retail
- 43rd Ave / Alexis Park Drive
- Kalamalka Lake Road corridor
- Okanagan Landing Road
- 24th–25th St industrial/service area
- BX / Silver Star Road foothill businesses
- Coldstream / Kalamalka Road
- Swan Lake corridor (Old Kamloops Rd / Hwy 97 north)
- Expansion zones: Armstrong core, Enderby core, Lumby core, Lake Country
  (Main St / Woodsdale / Berry Rd)

## Procedure

1. Pick the next unswept zone (closest first).
2. Search that zone's streets, plazas, and clusters for business listings.
3. Log every unique business found (name, category, approx location, website
   if shown) before moving on.
4. Continue through the grid until the requested number of unique businesses
   is reached — do not stop after the obvious first-page results.

## Notes

Hospitality zones, service clusters, and professional-office buildings hide
many non-obvious prospects — sweep them deliberately.
