# Partnership Fit Scorer

Score every prospect 1–10 for realistic partnership potential. The score
PRIORITIZES outreach — it does not eliminate opportunities. Even a 5/10
business still gets a custom email if the angle is valid. Do not reject
most businesses.

## Factors

- proximity to Back Nine Vernon
- local visibility / foot traffic
- customer overlap with Back Nine's clientele
- staff / team-building event potential
- customer-reward or gift potential
- in-facility advertising fit (TV screens, QR campaigns)
- event / prize / tournament / league sponsorship potential
- brand fit and community fit
- contact quality (direct email > form > social)
- website quality / active online presence
- likelihood of a useful conversation

## Rough bands

- 9–10: near-perfect overlap, strong contact, obvious mutual win — reach out first
- 7–8: strong angle, good contact — first wave
- 5–6: valid angle, weaker contact or fit — still email, second wave
- 3–4: marginal — include only with a genuinely plausible angle
- 1–2: inactive, out of area, or no honest angle — log as `bad fit`
