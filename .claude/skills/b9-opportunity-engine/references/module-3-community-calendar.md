# Module 3 — Community Calendar Engine

Scan public calendars and event sources within the run's time horizon for:

festivals · fundraisers · conferences · networking events · business luncheons
· tournaments · charity events · association events · tourism events · sports
events · community celebrations · trade events · award ceremonies · workshops
· seasonal events · public corporate events

## For each relevant event, capture where publicly available

- event name, organizer, venue, date, registration deadline
- sponsors and participating businesses (sponsor pages, event programs, PDFs)
- audience (who attends)

Never invent missing details. If a date or organizer can't be confirmed on an
official page, omit the field or the event.

## Do not relist calendars — connect them

For each event, work the chain and ask what it *creates* for Back Nine.
One event often yields several opportunities. Example — a local conference:

- private after-conference activity for attendees
- hotel referral partnership (where attendees stay)
- sponsor networking night
- corporate-event opportunity with the organizing company
- organizer partnership for next year's edition

Feed extracted organizers, sponsors, and participating businesses to Modules
1 and 2 as candidates.

## Relevance filter

Keep an event only if it plausibly leads to a corporate booking, partnership,
sponsorship, after-event gathering, referral flow, or visibility opportunity
for an indoor golf venue. A farmers' market with no organizer/sponsor angle is
noise; a business-awards gala is signal.

## Outreach timing

Anchor timing to the event lifecycle: sponsorship decisions close well before
the event; after-event activities are pitched 2–6 weeks out; organizer
partnerships are best raised shortly *after* a successful event. Classify into
COMMUNITY OPPORTUNITIES (or ACT NOW when a deadline falls within ~7 days).
