## Generation prompt scaffold

> Character reference sheet for [NAME], a [age] [build] golfer. [Face shape], [hair], [skin tone],
> [eye colour], [distinguishing features]. Wearing [wardrobe], [footwear], [accessories]; golf
> glove on the [hand] hand; [dominant] handed. 4K photorealistic, premium commercial, realistic
> skin texture, accurate anatomy, realistic hands and fingers, neutral studio lighting, clean
> background. Generate the listed views with IDENTICAL face, proportions, wardrobe, and scale in
> every view. POSITIVE LOCKS: identical identity, wardrobe, footwear, accessories, glove hand.
> DISTORTION-PREVENTION: no identity drift, no face warping, no age/skin/body/wardrobe changes,
> no extra or missing limbs, no extra or fused fingers, no broken joints, no plastic skin, no
> glowing eyes. NEGATIVE LOCKS: no golf ball in identity/expression views unless a scale view
> explicitly needs exactly one; never more than one golf ball.
