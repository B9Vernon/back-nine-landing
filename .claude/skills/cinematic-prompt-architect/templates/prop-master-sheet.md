## Generation prompt scaffold

> Prop reference set for [NAME], a [category] — [dimensions], [material], [colour], [branding],
> [condition]. 4K photorealistic, premium commercial, accurate materials, realistic metallic
> reflections, correct scale, correct contact shadows. Generate the listed views with IDENTICAL
> shape, scale, material, colour, and branding in every view. For golf clubs: straight shaft,
> correct shaft length, correct grip size, correct clubhead scale and attachment, correct
> clubface orientation, realistic lie and loft. POSITIVE LOCKS: identical shape/scale/material/
> colour/branding/orientation. DISTORTION-PREVENTION: no duplication, no disappearance, no
> scale/colour/shape change, no floating, no clipping through bodies; for clubs — no bent/twisted/
> melted/reversed clubheads, no wrong-way clubface, no grip fused into hand, no wrong-hand club.
> NEGATIVE LOCKS: if a golf ball appears for scale, exactly one; never more than one golf ball.
