# Visible Club-to-Ball Impact Verification (run before EVERY hitting-scene output)

Confirm each item before returning any prompt containing a club striking the ball. If any answer
is "no" or "uncertain," fix the prompt (simplify the camera, adjust framing/shutter, or split the
swing into shorter clips) before shipping. Full rules: `references/club-ball-impact.md`.

- [ ] Is the exact impact moment — clubface physically contacting the ball — clearly shown?
- [ ] Does the ball stay still/at address until contact (no early launch, movement, or teleport)?
- [ ] Does the ball leave the turf at contact and not remain behind after launch?
- [ ] Is the club never missing the ball (no whiff, no pass-through, no beside/above/below miss)?
- [ ] Is impact visible — not hidden by excessive blur, a cut, a flash, or an obstructed angle?
- [ ] Is the camera angle, framing, and shutter behaviour chosen specifically to keep impact
      readable?
- [ ] Is clubface orientation correct at contact?
- [ ] Are hand, wrist, arm, and body position correct at contact?
- [ ] Is there exactly one clearly readable clubface-to-ball contact (not multiple/ambiguous)?
- [ ] Is the compression/launch response and post-impact speed/spin/trajectory realistic?
- [ ] Does the club continue naturally through impact (follow-through), without freezing or
      clipping?
- [ ] Is there exactly one golf ball before, during, and after contact (cross-check with
      `checklists/single-ball-verification.md`)?

**Final gate:** IMPACT IS VISIBLE, PHYSICALLY CORRECT, AND INVOLVES EXACTLY ONE GOLF BALL.
