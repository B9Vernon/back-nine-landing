# Campaign-to-Recipient Mapper (shared skill)

For every opportunity, determine who should actually receive the campaign. The system
must never merely say "there is an event happening" — it must answer: **who already has
access to the people we want, and how does Neil reach them?**

## Questions to ask for every opportunity

- Who organized this?
- Who communicates with attendees?
- Where are the visitors staying?
- Which businesses already serve this audience?
- Which venues or groups control access to the audience?
- Which partners could forward, display, or mention this offer?
- Which contact has the highest chance of producing bookings?

## Priority ranking

| Priority | Recipients |
|---|---|
| PRIORITY 1 | Primary organizer or decision-maker. |
| PRIORITY 2 | Businesses or accommodations with direct access to the audience. |
| PRIORITY 3 | Teams, managers, vendors, venues, sponsors, or related local businesses. |
| PRIORITY 4 | General awareness channels: social, Google Business, in-facility TV screens, existing customers. |

## Gatekeepers by opportunity type

**Sports tournaments:** tournament organizers, league administrators, team managers,
coaches, sports association contacts, arena/facility contacts, hotels where teams may
stay, public parent or team contact paths, connected sponsors.

**SilverStar traffic:** accommodation hosts, chalet managers, property managers,
hotel/lodge contacts, guest-services contacts, tourism operators, shuttle and
transportation companies, visitor-facing restaurants and businesses.

**Conferences / corporate events:** event organizers, venue managers, hotel sales
managers, company event planners, chamber or association contacts, sponsor contacts,
nearby restaurants or hosts.

**Weddings / group weekends:** wedding planners, venues, photographers, hotels,
vacation-rental hosts, transportation companies, public event vendors.

**Weather, smoke, or bad outdoor conditions:** the campaign may be better suited to
social media, Google Business Profile, in-facility screens, the existing customer email
list, partner emails, and hotel/accommodation partners rather than cold outreach.
