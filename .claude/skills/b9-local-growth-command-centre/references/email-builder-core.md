# Email Builder Core (shared by every module)

The locked email structure, tone, and formatting used across the entire command centre.

## Process for every email

1. Research the recipient's website or public online presence.
2. Understand what they do.
3. Identify their audience.
4. Extract one or two useful personalizing details.
5. Choose the best Back Nine angle — the strongest one for this recipient, not a list.
6. Write one custom email subject.
7. Write one custom email body.
8. Open the body with: `Hey, I'm Neil.`
9. End with a simple reply question.
10. Stop before any signature.

## LOCKED RULES

- Every email body opens exactly: **Hey, I'm Neil.**
- Never write "Hey, I'm Vernon." Never introduce Neil as the city or the business.
- No email signature of any kind. Do not include: phone number, website footer,
  address, "Best regards", "Thanks," followed by Neil's name, name/title/signature
  block, Gmail-style signature, or any footer. Gmail already contains Neil's correct
  signature.
- End naturally with a simple reply question, then stop. Example ending:
  "Would you be open to a quick conversation about something like this?"

## Output format

```
NAME:
EMAIL:
EMAIL SUBJECT:
EMAIL BODY:
```

For event campaigns, `EVENT:` and `CAMPAIGN ANGLE:` may be added, but the core email
block stays clean.

## Email qualities

Every email must be: custom, researched, local, concise, warm, confident, useful,
specific to the recipient, and easy to reply to. Never: spammy, overly sales-driven,
generic, or filled with fake urgency. The email should clearly show the recipient's
website, business, event, accommodation, or organization was actually reviewed.

## Tone

Local, natural, premium, confident, friendly, helpful, specific. Not pushy, not
desperate, not corporate-sounding, not generic.

Do not over-explain Back Nine. Do not list every possible partnership angle. Pick the
strongest angle for that recipient.
