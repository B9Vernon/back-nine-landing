# Vacation Rental Module

Command: `RUN VACATION RENTAL ENGINE` (runs only this module).

## Job

Find vacation-rental hosts, accommodation owners, guest-suite operators, property
managers, chalet owners, boutique stays, cabins, B&Bs, and short-term rental operators
near Back Nine Vernon, then create custom host-facing emails.

## The pitch

NOT: "Advertise Back Nine for us."
IS: "Give your guests a better stay in Vernon with a premium local experience they can
enjoy while they are here."

The email is written to the host or accommodation operator. Neil sends it manually.
The host deals with their own guests — Back Nine never contacts guests through this
engine.

## Search order

1. Vernon accommodations first — closest to Back Nine Vernon.
2. Surrounding Vernon vacation rentals, guest suites, lake-area stays.
3. SilverStar accommodations as a premium secondary target.
4. Wider North Okanagan only after Vernon and SilverStar are covered.

SilverStar is high-value: guests often have higher disposable income, stay multiple
nights, travel in families or groups, and look for premium off-mountain experiences.
For SilverStar properties the angle should usually be: *"Give your guests a premium
Vernon activity when they want something different off the hill."*

## Target types

Vacation rental hosts, Airbnb-style hosts with public business contact information,
VRBO-style operators with public contact information, direct-booking vacation rentals,
guest suites, cabins, chalets, SilverStar accommodations, lake rentals, boutique stays,
bed and breakfasts, furnished short-stay rentals, property managers, accommodation
businesses, small inns, lodges, tourism-listed accommodations.

## Hard boundaries

Do not depend on Airbnb hidden data. Do not message hosts as a guest. Do not scrape
private Airbnb data. Use only public business contact paths (Public Contact Finder).

## Offer placeholder

Unless Neil provides a confirmed offer, use exactly:

```
{{PREFERRED_GUEST_DISCOUNT_OR_BONUS}}
```

## Angle themes to connect subtly

Better guest experience, standing out from other accommodations, easy local
recommendations, rainy-day / smoky-day / winter / evening activities, family and group
activities, memorable stays, stronger guest satisfaction, and the kind of thoughtful
perk that can help lead to better reviews.

**Never promise 5-star reviews. Never guarantee review improvement.**
