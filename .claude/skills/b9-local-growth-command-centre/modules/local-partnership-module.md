# Local Partnership Module

**This module preserves the existing B9 Opportunity & Partnership Engine and its
proven website-research-to-custom-email workflow. Do not rebuild, weaken, simplify, or
change what is already producing results.**

Command: `RUN PARTNERSHIP ENGINE` (runs only this module).

## Job

Find local businesses around Back Nine Vernon and create custom partnership emails,
starting from the closest radius and moving outward (see radius-first rule in
SKILL.md).

## The proven workflow (preserve exactly)

1. Discover a local business (radius-first).
2. Run the Duplicate and Relationship Guard against the B9 Growth Database and the
   legacy CSVs (`back_nine_vernon_prospect_database.csv`, `Fable 1 Contacts.csv`).
3. Research the business's actual website / public presence — what they do, who their
   customers are, one or two personalizing details.
4. Find the best public contact path (Public Contact Finder).
5. Choose the single strongest Back Nine angle for that specific business.
6. Write the custom email via the Email Builder Core (locked rules apply).
7. Record the prospect in the B9 Growth Database.
8. Deliver via the Outreach File Module.

## Think wide, not narrow

Back Nine Vernon can potentially partner with almost any legitimate local business.
Do not only look for golf-related businesses or businesses that already mention
sponsorships. Available angles include:

advertising inside the facility, TV screen advertising, QR-code campaigns,
team-building events, corporate golf nights, staff appreciation, client appreciation,
customer rewards, prize sponsorship, tournament sponsorship, league sponsorship, gift
experiences, referral partnerships, co-branded local campaigns, family and group
experiences, social nights, employee wellness, private events, local brand exposure,
community connection.

The question to ask for every business:

> What can Back Nine Vernon offer this specific business that would help their staff,
> customers, clients, visibility, or community connection?

Pick the strongest single angle — never list them all in the email.
