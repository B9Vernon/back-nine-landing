WHAT THE EMAILS SAY ABOUT TIMING — AND WHAT THEY DON'T
They lean on things that are actually true for everyone: Halloween is close,
the outdoor courses are closing for the season, December is coming, and Back
Nine is indoors and open 24/7 all year. They do NOT tell anyone what their own
plans are. None of them says "you're planning your party" — every party
mention is conditional ("if a Christmas or year-end night is on the list").
None of them claims December is filling up, because nothing verified says so.

NOTHING HERE IS OFFERED FREE. Party bookings are quoted by the group. No food,
bar or catering is promised — none of that is confirmed for the venue, so it
is left for you to raise on the reply.
