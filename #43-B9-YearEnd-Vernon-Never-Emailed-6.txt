Year-end parties — 11 more Vernon businesses you've never emailed
Prepared 2026-10-25. 11 emails, all paste straight into Gmail.

WHY 11, NOT 20
Vernon has very few businesses left that publish an email and haven't
heard from you. I looked at about 110 more names this time. Most were
already in #24–#42 (often from #28 and #29), had no email anywhere, or
turned out to be in Kelowna, Salmon Arm, Armstrong or further. I stopped
at 11 rather than pad the list. See "What next" at the bottom.

WHERE THESE 11 CAME FROM
All 11 are in Vernon (one builder also gives a Coldstream address). New
sources this time:
  - Vernon job postings (Job Bank, Indeed, Glassdoor), which turned up
    employers with crews that weren't on the log: #1, #3, #4
  - the home builders' association directory (CHBA Central Okanagan):
    #5–#8
  - the Fairweather Road industrial area: #2
  - tree services and the rest of the July list: #9–#11
Every address was found on the business's own website, its Facebook
page or a directory listing. None was made up.

Same rule as #27–#42: golfers, mostly men, about 30 people or fewer, and
no societies, non-profits, seniors' homes or schools.

THE WORDING
Same as #27–#42. Every email starts with "My name is Neil.", asks "Does
having your Christmas or year-end party at Back Nine interest you?",
compares a restaurant with private bays, and ends on a 24/7 line. All 11
say "on 45th Avenue here in Vernon".

HOW I CHECKED THAT THEY'RE NEW TO YOUR INBOX
Each of the 11 was checked against:
  - every email in #24 to #42
  - your Sent folder, by exact address, by company domain and by company
    name, back to June
  - your inbox, in case any of them had written to you first
None of the 11 turned up.
