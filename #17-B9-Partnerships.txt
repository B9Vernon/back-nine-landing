WHAT WAS BLOCKING DISCOVERY, AND WHAT CHANGED

Runs 12-16 delivered 14, 14, 20, 3 and 2 prospects. The cause was not a
shortage of Vernon businesses. Since run 12 every entry needs a verified
published email, and each one costs a dedicated search — but candidates were
being checked against the log ONE AT A TIME, after that search had already
been spent. Most turned out to be businesses already contacted, so the run
ran out of budget long before it ran out of town.

Two fixes, both made inside the existing engine:

  tools/screen_candidates.py   NEW. Screens an entire harvest against all
                               1,861 logged businesses at once, on every
                               duplicate axis, BEFORE a single email search is
                               spent. Harvest three hundred names, pay only
                               for the ones that can actually ship.

  b9lib address axis           REPAIRED. A civic address stood for every
                               tenant in the building, so KAL Fitness
                               (11-100 Kalamalka Lake Rd) was reported as a
                               duplicate of Chemac Industries (100 Kalamalka
                               Lake Rd) — different units, unrelated
                               businesses. Vernon is full of multi-tenant
                               plazas, so this was quietly hiding real
                               prospects. Units are now compared; an address
                               with no unit still matches everything at it, so
                               no duplicate protection was traded away.

Kal Fitness is entry 1 in this run because of that second fix.
