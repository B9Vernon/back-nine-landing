# Email rules

Email content is generated only for manually approved companies. Generation
stores text locally; it does not export or send anything.

## Required opening

Every body starts exactly:

```text
Hey [Company Name],

I'm Neil, owner of Back Nine Golf here in Vernon.
```

## Required content

- A natural reference to the real hiring signal or multiple openings
- Publicly supported company context
- Why current hiring makes the timing relevant
- Three to five matched Back Nine offers
- An invitation to visit Back Nine for a quick look
- Usually 150 to 250 words

## Voice

Use premium, modern, personal, local, confident, approachable, direct language.
Be specific without pretending Neil knows the recipient.

## Prohibited content

- A sign-off
- Fake familiarity or an invented relationship
- Guessed facts, contacts, or email addresses
- "Hope this email finds you well"
- "To whom it may concern" or "Dear Sir/Madam"
- "We are reaching out"
- Mentions of scraping, an engine, scores, or every job platform
- Desperate, pushy, generic, or overly polished corporate wording

Subjects are short and contextual, such as a team, staff-perk, or client-hosting
idea for the named company.
