# Discover public contacts

1. Prefer public owner/GM, HR, sales/marketing, events, then general business email.
2. Accept an address only when it appears in imported public evidence or on an official public page.
3. Never derive, pattern-match, or guess an email address.
4. Use a contact-form URL when no public email exists; retain phone only for manual follow-up.
5. Exclude missing-contact companies from TXT unless the operator explicitly includes them.

Input: accepted company and official/imported public evidence.

Output: best contact type/value/source plus audit outcome.
