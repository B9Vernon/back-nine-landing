# Match Back Nine offers

1. Start with the classified role category and multi-posting signal.
2. Select only offers that fit how the company serves staff, clients, customers, or guests.
3. Prefer three to five specific ideas; add a new-hire welcome concept for active team growth.
4. Do not invent products outside the configured Back Nine offer set.
5. Feed offer names to the email generator without internal scoring.

Input: `JobCategory` and multiple-posting flag.

Output: ordered offer names, maximum five.
