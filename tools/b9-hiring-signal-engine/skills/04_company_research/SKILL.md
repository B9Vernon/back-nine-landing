# Research companies

1. Prefer an imported official website or an operator-supplied website map.
2. Require `--live` before fetching and use the safe web client.
3. Inspect the home, about, team, service, career, contact, and location pages when permitted.
4. Verify that site identity matches the company before accepting live research.
5. Store sources and an internal summary; never place research notes in final TXT.

Input: accepted companies with official website evidence.

Output: internal summary, official URL/domain, sources, and uncertainty status.
