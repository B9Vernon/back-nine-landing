4. VERNON WINTER CARNIVAL — HOST AN OFFICIAL EVENT · DEADLINE OCT 31
   The best new find in this run, and neither of the two previous
   opportunity runs caught it.
   - What it is: ten days each February, the largest winter carnival of its
     kind in Western Canada. The 66th ran Feb 6-15, 2026 ("Team Carnival —
     Canada Goes for Gold"). The 67th is expected early February 2027; dates

   - Why it matters to Back Nine specifically: the Carnival does not run most

   - Official website: vernonwintercarnival.com/host-an-event/
