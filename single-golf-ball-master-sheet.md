## Generation prompt scaffold

> Reference set for [NAME], a single premium golf ball — [diameter], [colour], [dimple pattern],
> [surface texture], [markings], [condition]. 4K photorealistic, premium commercial, accurate
> material response, realistic reflections and contact shadows, correct scale. Generate the listed
> views. EACH IMAGE CONTAINS EXACTLY ONE GOLF BALL. POSITIVE LOCKS: identical size, colour, dimple
> pattern, markings, and material in every view. SINGLE-BALL-ONLY LOCK: never render a second,
> extra, ghost, or duplicated ball anywhere in any view, including reflections, the tee/turf
> surface, or the clubface scale view; the "beside a clubface for scale" view shows ONE ball only.
> Motion-blur reference (if included) must read as one ball plus abstract light/air, never as two.
> DISTORTION-PREVENTION: no size/colour/marking change, no split, no teleport, no transformation.
> NEGATIVE LOCKS: no pile, cluster, basket, or row of balls; never more than one golf ball.
