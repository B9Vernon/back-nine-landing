WHY 18 AND NOT 25

allows and found 18 that fit and have a published email. I stopped there
because the searches had almost stopped finding anything new.

About 70 of the businesses I found had already been emailed. In Vernon,
the trades are now almost completely covered: roofing, auto repair,
cabinets, masonry, flooring, bike shops, barbershops, used cars and
mortgage brokers came back already contacted, nearly every one. What's
still new is mostly small professional offices (engineers and
environmental consultants), which turned up in public professional lists,
plus a few shops on Cylex and Hotfrog and some crews out in Coldstream,
Armstrong and Lumby. Every address in this file was copied from a page
that showed it. None was made up.
